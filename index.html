Operating Model & PMO Design

Building the cadences, governance forums, and decision frameworks that let product and technology organizations execute at scale.

Most product and technology organizations don't lack talent — they lack the system that turns talent into coordinated outcomes. I design the planning rhythms, intake and prioritization processes, delivery standards, and accountability structures that give Product, Engineering, and executive leadership clarity on priorities, trade-offs, and outcomes.

ProofBuilt Clarivate's Product & Technology PMO from the ground up — unifying six fragmented teams across four business units, growing from 25 to 45, covering 1,000+ technologists and 60+ products.

Portfolio Governance & Investment Strategy

Strategic prioritization, investment trade-off analysis, and capacity-aligned planning across complex R&D portfolios.

When the portfolio gets too big to govern by intuition, leadership needs the analysis, scenario modeling, and decision frameworks that connect strategic intent to capacity and capital. I run the diagnostic, build the modeling, and surface the trade-offs that let executive teams reset investment allocation with confidence — not just for one budget cycle, but as a durable operating capability.

ProofLed $400M+ portfolio rationalization across 200+ products post-acquisition — shifting strategic allocation from 10% to 35% and resetting product direction for years forward.

AI Program Leadership & Integration

Governing AI initiatives to production at scale — and applying AI hands-on to operating model, planning, and decision support.

Most organizations are still learning the difference between AI strategy decks and AI in production. I bring both: the governance frameworks that let cross-geography teams ship AI initiatives responsibly at speed, and the hands-on practitioner's view of what AI actually means for how product and technology operations get done. The combination is rarer than it should be — and it's the difference between AI as a slide and AI as a working capability.

Transformation Through Change

Operating continuity through M&A, leadership transitions, and major reorganizations — the work most organizations underestimate until momentum is already gone.

M&A integration, leadership turnover, and major reorganizations expose seams that work fine under stable conditions. I step in as the operating partner who holds delivery discipline, decision cadence, and team focus through the disruption — keeping execution moving while the organization works through the bigger changes. The goal isn't to prevent change; it's to make sure the change doesn't cost the momentum you've already built.

ProofPartnered with three successive CPOs through the CPA Global acquisition by Clarivate — holding operating continuity from diligence through Day 1 readiness and post-merger integration across multiple business units.
